## Template for an entry

### <Entity> - <short title of the finding>

- **Documented problem**: who hit it, in what task, under what conditions. Separate symptom from cause.
- **Their solution**: the mechanism, and alternatives they discarded (only if stated).
- **Known limits**: what the author admits it does not solve.
- **Result reported**: what changed and how it was measured. If it is only a claim, label it as such.
- **Availability**: announced / limited access / beta / generally available / unknown. Which interface or demo was actually observed.
- **Our users' need**: which LayerLens persona and open question (`LAYERLENS_CONTEXT.md`) this touches.
- **LayerLens alternative (hypothesis, pending validation)**: the difference in behavior we could offer, and for whom it would be better and by how much.
- **How to validate**: the comparison, on the same task and conditions, that would test it. What result would make us drop the idea.
- **Action**: adopt / explore alternative / investigate / do nothing.
- **Evidence links and dates**: canonical URL(s), discovery URL, publication and read dates.
