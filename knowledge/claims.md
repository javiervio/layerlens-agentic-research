## Template for a claim

### C-0001: short statement of what we believe

- **Statement**: precise claim, with its scope.
- **Source(s)**: [Title](canonical-url), author or org, date, read scope (full / sections / abstract).
- **Discovered via**: feed / search / citation / post / Javier.
- **Evidence label**: documented capability / bounded empirical / independent corroboration / proposal / anecdotal / incomplete access.
- **Limits**: what this does not establish.
- **Relates to**: other claim ids; whether it confirms, extends, contradicts, or supersedes them.
- **LayerLens relevance**: which open question in `LAYERLENS_CONTEXT.md` it speaks to (by number), or "general."
- **Confidence**: stated with reasons, not a percentage.
- **Recheck after**: date, per the review cadence in `PROTOCOL.md`.
