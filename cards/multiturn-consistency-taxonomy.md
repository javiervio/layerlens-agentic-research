## Claims
- C-0015: Multi-turn consistency measured over 20-step tasks across 84,540 trajectories and 8 model families, using survival analysis (time-to-first-failure) and a 7-category failure-rationale taxonomy (kappa=0.83). Failures have distinct "fingerprints" by model and context; early failures impulse-driven, later ones fatigue/cost-benefit-framed; longer deliberation correlated with more intra-rationale contradiction.
